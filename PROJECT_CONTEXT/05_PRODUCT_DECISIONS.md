# Product Decisions

This document records major product decisions that have been finalized.

It is the authoritative record of **why** the product behaves the way it does.

Do not record implementation details here.

Do not record temporary ideas.

Only record decisions that have been agreed upon.

# Approved Decisions

---

## Delta is a Knowledge Operating System

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

Foundation

### Decision

Delta is a Knowledge Operating System for Architecture Projects.

### Reason

Architects need a system that transforms fragmented project information into structured knowledge rather than another project management tool.

### Alternatives Considered

- Project Management Platform
- Document Management System
- Chat Application

### Impact

All future features must strengthen the knowledge lifecycle.

### Reversible

No

---

## Evidence First

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

Foundation

### Decision

Every piece of project information begins as evidence before becoming knowledge.

### Reason

Knowledge must be curated and traceable.

### Alternatives Considered

Automatic knowledge creation without review.

### Impact

Review Packages remain central to Delta.

### Reversible

No

---

## Meetings are First-Class Evidence

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

3.5

### Decision

Meetings are treated as primary evidence objects.

### Reason

Most project decisions originate from discussions and meetings.

### Alternatives Considered

Treat meetings as attachments.

### Impact

Meetings receive dedicated interfaces and workflows.

### Reversible

No

---

## Project Evolution is Historical

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

3.5

### Decision

Project Evolution records only verified knowledge milestones.

Future milestones are never displayed.

### Reason

Project Evolution represents knowledge history, not project scheduling.

### Alternatives Considered

- Timeline with future milestones.
- Timeline generated from payment schedules.
- Timeline generated from project programme.

### Impact

Evolution becomes a trustworthy historical record.

### Reversible

Yes

---

## Delta is a Continuous Workspace

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

3.6

### Decision

Users should remain inside one continuous workspace whenever possible.

### Reason

Architects should not constantly navigate between pages.

### Alternatives Considered

Traditional multi-page SaaS navigation.

### Impact

Contextual panels are preferred over new pages.

### Reversible

Yes

---

## Delta Branding

### Status

Approved

### Sprint

3.6

### Decision

Replace generic "AI" terminology with "Delta".

Examples:

- Ask Delta
- Delta Review
- Delta Summary
- Delta Recommendation
- Delta Confidence

### Reason

Delta is part of the product identity rather than a generic AI assistant.

### Alternatives Considered

Continue using generic AI terminology.

### Impact

Creates a consistent product identity.

### Reversible

Yes
